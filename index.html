Coresets & data compression

Keeping what matters.

I work on small representations of large datasets that preserve the information needed to solve a problem. My research focuses on coresets for clustering and efficient algorithms for estimation.

Game theory & fairness

Sharing resources fairly.

I study how to allocate resources fairly when budgets, capacities, and other constraints come into play, and design algorithms with provable fairness guarantees.

Currently Exploring the intersection of AI alignment and mechanism design, including the behavior of LLMs in markets.
